## Brand & Style

### Personality & Mission
The design system establishes an authoritative, high-precision aesthetic tailored for tier-one architectural, engineering, and government procurement stakeholders across Egypt and Saudi Arabia. It bridges robust industrial engineering (telescopic platforms, high-load bleachers, acoustic auditorium seating) with elite architectural elegance. The interface communicates rock-solid reliability, regulatory compliance, structural permanence, and Swiss-grade engineering rigor.

### Target Audience
- Chief architects and acoustic consultants specifying large-scale theatres and auditoriums.
- Sports infrastructure contractors, project directors, and stadium operations managers.
- Institutional procurement heads across the GCC (KSA Vision 2030 megaprojects) and Egyptian national infrastructure initiatives.

### Emotional Response
The experience instills confidence, architectural grandeur, absolute precision, and bespoke engineering capability.

### Aesthetic Direction: Modern Architectural Constructivism
A fusion of corporate precision and structural engineering minimalism:
- **Visual Signature**: A solid royal blue square accent block (`w-3 h-3` to `w-4 h-4`) anchor preceding section titles, serving as a structural datum line.
- **RTL-First Architecture**: Native support for bi-directional layouts, optimized for formal Arabic engineering terminology with uncompromised Latin technical spec alignments.
- **Materiality**: Crisp hairline engineering grids, stark white-to-slate contrast, subtle mechanical elevations, and high-visibility tabular data blocks.

## Colors

The color palette reflects institutional authority, technical competence, and high-spec architectural engineering.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#2B50E8` - Royal Precision Blue)**: The commanding anchor color. Drives primary calls-to-action, active specification highlights, brand identity accents, and the signature title square blocks.
- **Secondary (`#0F2A6B` - Deep Structural Navy)**: Grounding tone representing industrial strength, steel fabrication, and enterprise depth. Used for high-contrast headers, dense structural backgrounds, technical matrices, and footer slabs.
- **Tertiary (`#1BB3D1` - Engineering Cyan)**: Accent color evoking laser levels, CAD viewports, architectural draft markers, and system readiness. Deployed for micro-badges, live status pings, dynamic measurement callouts, and key performance metric highlights.
- **Neutral Primary (`#0B1528` - Carbon Slate)**: Deep technical ink for razor-sharp typography and high-legibility specs.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` Pure White & `#F3F4F6` Cloud Gray)**: Provide technical clarity. `#F3F4F6` serves as an alternate surface layer for CAD blueprint areas, dimension charts, and nested spec containers.

### Contrast & Functional Roles
- **Border Hairlines (`#E2E8F0` / `#CBD5E1`)**: Used extensively to mimic architectural plan drawings and technical section dividers.
- **Status Validation**: Success `#059669` (Structural Compliance), Warning `#D97706` (Load Constraint), Error `#DC2626` (Specification Mismatch).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy accommodates full bilingual parity (Arabic & English), prioritizing clarity for dense engineering data, load metrics, and architectural submittals.

### Font System & Bi-Directionality
- **Display & Headlines (`Montserrat`)**: Delivers geometric authority and architectural precision. For Arabic titles, the system maps automatically to **Cairo** or **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**, maintaining matched optical x-height and bold baseline structure.
- **Body (`IBM Plex Sans` / `IBM Plex Sans Arabic`)**: Systematic, clean, and legible across continuous technical documentation, tender specifications, and tender proposals.
- **Engineering Specs & Numbers (`Inter`)**: Set strictly with **Tabular Figures (`tnum`)** and slash zero features enabled. Guarantees that structural measurements (kN/m², dB ratings, dimensional spans, seat centers in mm) align reliably across rows and dynamic quotation tables.

### Typesetting Rules
- Section titles must pair with the brand signature square, vertically aligned to the capital height of the typography.
- Arabic text maintains adequate line height multipliers (+15% compared to Latin equivalents) to prevent diacritic clipping.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy: The Architectural Grid
The layout follows a modular 12-column structural grid system built on an 8px base rhythm. Content mirrors naturally along the vertical axis when switching between LTR (English) and RTL (Arabic) without visual distortion.

### Breakpoints & Layout Adaptability
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid grid. Gutter: `1rem`, Outer Margin: `1.25rem`. Complex technical comparison matrices collapse into vertically stacked spec cards or horizontally swipeable spec carousels.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8-column grid. Gutter: `1.5rem`, Outer Margin: `2.5rem`. Side-by-side seating configuration tools switch to split view.
- **Desktop (1025px – 1440px)**: 12-column fixed grid with a max-width of `1360px`. Gutter: `2rem`, Outer Margin: `auto`.
- **Wide Screens (> 1440px)**: Max content container constrained to `1400px` to maintain strict visual line-lengths for engineering reading clarity.

### Component Spacing Rhythm
- Compact data rows and spec pills use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).
- Component card interiors, inputs, and modular engineering blocks use `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px).
- Major structural page sections maintain generous industrial breathing room with `space-xl` (40px) up to 80px gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

### Physicality: Clean Architectural Precision
This design system avoids heavy decorative drop shadows or unfocused blurs. Elevation is conveyed through razor-sharp mechanical borders, surface level contrasts, and tight, tactile micro-shadows that mirror structural drafting software.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Base Blueprint Plane - `#FFFFFF` / `#F3F4F6`)**: The static canvas. Neutral, non-elevated ground.
2. **Level 1 (Structural Component Panels)**: Framed with a 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient, low-spread drop shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(11, 21, 40, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(11, 21, 40, 0.03);`
3. **Level 2 (Active Cards & Focus States)**: Applied during hover or selection of seating models and telescopic modules. Features a crisp border transition to `#2B50E8` and an elevated shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(15, 42, 107, 0.08), 0 2px 4px rgba(15, 42, 107, 0.04);`
4. **Level 3 (Engineering Drawers, Modals & Floating Spec Sheets)**: Overlay containers for CAD downloads, load-bearing calculators, and BIM file viewers:
   `box-shadow: 0 20px 35px -8px rgba(11, 21, 40, 0.16), 0 8px 12px -4px rgba(11, 21, 40, 0.08);`

### Glassmorphism Constraint
Backdrop blurs are reserved solely for fixed top navigation bars (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92);`) to maintain structural readability during deep document scrolling.

## Shapes

### Form Language: Engineered Rigidity
The shape language draws inspiration from industrial steel extrusions, acoustic panel bevels, and rigid telescopic platform edges. Elements feature controlled, low-radius curvature (`0.25rem` / 4px base) to retain an architectural, machined aesthetic without visual softness.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Base Components (4px - `rounded`)**: Buttons, text input fields, drop-down menus, spec tags, and technical table cells.
- **Containers & Panels (8px - `rounded-lg`)**: Engineering cards, product showcase frames, specification comparison matrices, and BIM preview containers.
- **Overlays (12px - `rounded-xl`)**: Heavy engineering modal dialogs and quote generation sheets.
- **Visual Signature Square (0px)**: The section title brand accent square is strictly non-rounded (`rounded-none`), underscoring precision measurement.

## Components

### 1. Section Title Signature Indicator
- **Construction**: Every major section headline (`headline-lg`, `headline-md`) is preceded by a solid Royal Blue (`#2B50E8`) square (`14px × 14px` on desktop, `10px × 10px` on mobile).
- **Positioning**: In LTR, placed to the immediate left with `margin-right: 0.75rem`. In RTL, placed to the immediate right with `margin-left: 0.75rem`. Vertically aligned to the headline's cap-height.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary Technical CTA**: Solid `#2B50E8` background, white label, 4px border-radius, minimum height 48px for tactile mobile target acquisition. Hover states transition to `#0F2A6B` with a subtle elevation shift.
- **Secondary (Tender / Spec Submittal)**: Transparent background, 1.5px solid border in `#0F2A6B`, text `#0F2A6B`. Hover state applies `#F3F4F6` surface tint.
- **BIM/CAD Action Button**: `#0F2A6B` background with `#1BB3D1` icon accent, used exclusively for technical asset downloads (.RVT, .DWG, .IFC).

### 3. Engineering Cards (Product & Bleacher Systems)
- **Structure**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, `rounded-lg` (8px). 
- **Header**: Features high-contrast photography of installed seating/bleachers, accompanied by a top-corner category chip.
- **Data Sub-panel**: Embedded bottom module in `#F3F4F6` displaying key metrics (e.g., Row Spacing, Acoustic Class, Fire Rating NFPA/EN) structured using tabular figures (`Inter`).

### 4. Technical Spec Chips & Status Badges
- **Specification Chips**: `#F3F4F6` background, `#0B1528` text, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, set in `label-caps`.
- **Engineering Highlights**: Light `#1BB3D1` background tint (10% opacity) with solid `#0F2A6B` text and `#1BB3D1` left-border accent bar (right-border in RTL).

### 5. Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Form Fields**: Clean white surface with an explicit 1.5px `#CBD5E1` border. Active focus triggers a solid `#2B50E8` outline with 0px blur ring. Labels are placed above inputs in `IBM Plex Sans` (Semi-bold, 13px).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Machined geometric profile (4px radius for checkboxes, circle for radios). Active fill uses `#2B50E8` with high-contrast white indicators.

### 6. Engineering Metric Matrices (Comparison Tables)
- Alternating subtle row backgrounds (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8FAFC`).
- 1px hairline dividers (`#E2E8F0`).
- Dedicated column headers styled in deep Navy (`#0F2A6B`) with sharp, high-contrast white text.
- Full RTL bidirectional flipping for Egyptian and Saudi market submittals.
